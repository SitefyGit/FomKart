FomKart Global Marketplace Payment & Settlement
Workflow
Business Model
FomKart operates as a global marketplace. Customers make payments to FomKart, and FomKart
manages settlements to sellers after successful order completion and the applicable
holding/dispute period.
Commission Structure
• FomKart Commission: 20% Flat
• Seller Share: 80%


Payment Flow
1. Customer places an order and pays through the integrated payment gateway.
2. FomKart receives 100% of the payment.
3. Funds are held in the platform settlement account/wallet.
4. Order is fulfilled by the seller.
5. Refund/dispute period is checked.
6. FomKart deducts its 20% commission.
7. Remaining amount is released to the seller wallet.
8. Seller can withdraw funds to supported payout methods.


Example Settlement
Customer Payment: $100
FomKart Commission (20%): $20
Seller Receives: $80
